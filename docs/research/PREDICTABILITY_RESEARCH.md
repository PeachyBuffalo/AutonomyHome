# Predictability Metrics — Manual Research Guide

Variance approval rate, rezoning rate, zoning amendments, and litigation require manual research from primary sources.

---

## 1. Variance Approval Rate (5-year)

**Definition:** % of variance applications approved by the Zoning Board of Appeals or equivalent over the past 5 years.

**Sources:**
- Planning Commission minutes
- Zoning Board of Appeals minutes
- Township/city website → Meetings → Archives

**Process:**
1. Locate minutes for ZBA or body that hears variance requests
2. For each meeting, count: applications received, approved, denied
3. Sum over 5 years (e.g., 2019–2024)
4. Rate = approved / (approved + denied) × 100

**Example search:** "[Jurisdiction] Zoning Board of Appeals minutes 2019"

---

## 2. Rezoning Approval Rate

**Definition:** % of rezoning/rezoning-related applications approved.

**Sources:**
- Planning Commission minutes
- Township Board minutes (if they approve rezonings)
- Meeting packets (often have application summaries)

**Process:**
1. Find meetings where rezoning was on the agenda
2. Count approved vs denied
3. Rate = approved / total × 100

---

## 3. Zoning Amendments (10-year)

**Definition:** Count of zoning ordinance amendments over 10 years.

**Sources:**
- Zoning ordinance document (check amendment history)
- Township clerk records
- Meeting minutes (ordinance adoption)

**Process:**
1. Obtain current zoning ordinance
2. Check "Amendment History" or similar section
3. Count amendments from 2014–2024
4. If no history, search minutes for "ordinance" + "zoning" adoptions

---

## 4. Zoning-Related Litigation (10-year)

**Definition:** Count of court cases involving the jurisdiction and zoning/land use.

**Sources:**
- [Michigan Courts Case Search](https://www.courts.michigan.gov/)
- Ottawa County Circuit Court
- Search by party name (township/city name)

**Process:**
1. Go to courts.michigan.gov
2. Case search → Ottawa County Circuit Court
3. Search party: "[Jurisdiction] Township" or "[Jurisdiction] Charter Township"
4. Filter by case type (civil)
5. Manually review for zoning/land use relevance
6. Count 2014–2024

**Note:** Time-intensive. Consider sampling or focusing on high-profile cases.
